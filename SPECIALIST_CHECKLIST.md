# TeachSim: instructional validation checklist (provisional)

Please ask an SI specialist to check, for **each** of the eight fictional scenarios:

- [ ] Each target describes an **observable completed response**, not only initiation.
- [ ] Every step has a clear discriminative cue and appropriately arranged materials.
- [ ] A genuine independent opportunity and response interval is planned.
- [ ] The gestural → model hierarchy is suitable for the step and learner, and a reliable controlling prompt has been identified where required. Do not assume modelling is always sufficient.
- [ ] Reinforcement is defined separately from prompt levels, including response to correct independent and prompted attempts.
- [ ] The procedure for an **incorrect response** is defined; this is intentionally not simulated yet.
- [ ] Data codes distinguish independent, gestural, model and incomplete responses.
- [ ] Prompt fading, maintenance and generalisation plans are considered for later development.
- [ ] Physical prompting, if appropriate, has trained staff, student-specific safeguards and approval; it is not currently simulated.

Research foundations previously discussed: Jimenez, Browder & Trela (2007) on task-analysis training and teacher self-monitoring; Jimenez et al. on systematic instruction and generalisation; VCU Autism Center for Education systematic prompting resources. This checklist is an adaptation, not a published validated Jimenez instrument.
