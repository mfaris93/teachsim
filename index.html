Teaching procedure

Give a direction and wait for a response. If the learner does not respond or responds incorrectly, use the prescribed gestural prompt and wait. If needed, model and wait. Acknowledge completion or pause for review when the step is incomplete.

Trainer's worked example Reveals a sample sequence
  1. Give instruction: “Sam, put soap on your hands.”
  2. Wait and observe: Observe whether Sam applies soap.
  3. Gestural prompt (if needed): Point towards the soap dispenser, then wait again.
  4. Model prompt (if needed): Demonstrate “Apply soap”, then wait again.
  5. Respond to the outcome: If the learner completes the step, acknowledge it. Otherwise, pause and review the plan after modelling.

How TeachSim works

Practise Least-to-Most Prompting in fictional teaching tasks, then discuss your decisions with a trainer.

  1. Prepare — Choose a fictional scenario and a pathway. Review the learner profile, task steps, and prescribed procedure.
  2. Practise — Give an instruction and wait. Observe the learner’s response. After no response or an incorrect response, use the next planned prompt and observe again. Acknowledge success or pause an unresolved step.
  3. Review — After completing the episode, reflect on response opportunities, prompt escalation and acknowledgement.
