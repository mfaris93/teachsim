How TeachSim works

Practise a fictional teaching task, then discuss the review with your trainer. This is not a formal competence test.

  1. Prepare — Choose a fictional scenario and a pathway. Review the learner profile, task steps, and prescribed procedure.
  2. Practise — Give an instruction, allow a response opportunity, and choose how to prompt or acknowledge the learner. Each action changes a pre-authored learner response.
  3. Review — Only after the complete episode, compare your recorded sequence with the demonstration protocol, then reflect and retry.
Guided

Click a prepared direction; use the action controls. Learner responses are predictable.

Experienced

Deliver the direction independently, then select actions for varied learner patterns. Speech and timing are not measured.
