How TeachSim works

TeachSim is a practice rehearsal—not a test of real teaching competence. A trainer can introduce the procedure, observe your practice, and guide the debrief.

  1. Prepare — Choose a fictional scenario and a pathway. Review the learner profile, task steps, and prescribed procedure.
  2. Practise — Give an instruction, allow a response opportunity, and choose how to prompt or acknowledge the learner. Each action changes a pre-authored learner response.
  3. Review — Only after the complete episode, compare your recorded sequence with the demonstration protocol, then reflect and retry.
New · Guided

Click a prepared direction; use the action controls. Learner responses are predictable.

Experienced · Independent

Type your own direction; respond to more varied learner patterns. The wording itself is not scored.

This is a rule-based fictional simulation, not generative AI. Waiting is simulated and timing, voice delivery and physical prompting are not measured. Both pathways use the same provisional procedure.
